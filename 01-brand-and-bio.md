# 1 — Brand & Profile Setup

Your profile is your landing page. Visitors decide in ~3 seconds whether to follow or buy. This file gives you the exact words to paste in.

---

## Positioning (say this everywhere)

> **We're a two-person tech team: a software engineer and a cybersecurity specialist. We build your product, then attack it the way real attackers would — so your users never face one.**

Why this works: "team of two" = personal attention, "attack our own work" = instant credibility + memorable hook, no jargon.

**Who you serve (pick your focus):** small businesses, startups and non-technical founders who need software built AND secured, without hiring a full agency.

---

## The 3 profile fields that matter

### 1. Name field (this is SEARCHED by Instagram — use keywords, not just your name)

```
YourName | Software Dev & Cybersecurity
```

People search "cybersecurity" and "software developer" — this puts you in those results.

### 2. Bio — pick one (all under Instagram's 150-character limit)

**Option A — the hook (recommended):**
```
Software engineer × Cybersecurity specialist
We build your product — then attack it like a hacker would.
DM "PROJECT" for a free quote
```

**Option B — the menu:**
```
Dev + Security duo 🛡️
Apps • Automation • Pentesting
We protect your business — before someone else tests it 👇
```

**Option C — the straight shooter:**
```
Your tech team of two
Software that works. Security that holds.
Free 15-min chat ↓
```

### 3. Link — use the included `link-in-bio/index.html` (free to host on GitHub Pages or Netlify), not Linktree. Same look as your brand, zero monthly fee, and it's proof you can actually build things.

## Visual identity (already applied to every asset)

- **Colors:** near-black green-tinted `#060A07` background, terminal green `#4ADE80` primary accent, red `#F87171` = security/red-team, orange `#FB923C` = Python, amber `#FFBD2E` = highlights. **No blue or purple anywhere** (client rule).
- **Style:** dark, technical, terminal-inspired — reads as "serious tech" and matches both of your skills
- **Rule going forward:** every post you make yourself uses this dark background + one accent color. Consistency is what makes a page "look pro."
