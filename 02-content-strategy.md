# 2 — Content Strategy & 30-Day Calendar

Goal: turn visitors into DMs that say "how much for...?"

## The 4 pillars

Every post fits one pillar. A pro page has a *visible balance* — not random posts.

| Pillar | Share | Purpose | Format |
|---|---|---|---|
| **PROOF** — work & results | 30% | "They can actually do this" | Carousels, before/after, demos |
| **EDUCATION** — tips & insights | 30% | Saves + shares = new followers | Reels, tip posts like `07-tip.png` |
| **TRUST** — the humans | 25% | People hire people | Stories, BTS, opinions |
| **OFFER** — services & CTA | 15% | Converts followers to clients | `02-services.png`, `08-cta.png` |

## Cadence (sustainable for 2 people)

- **3–4 feed posts/week** (a carousel counts as one)
- **Stories daily** — even one poll or behind-the-scene keeps you "active"
- **2 reels/week minimum** — this is where NEW people find you; feed posts mostly reach people who already follow you

## 10 reel ideas that work for dev/security pages

1. "5 signs your website is hackable" (screen + talk)
2. "We tried to break into our own app — here's what happened"
3. "3 Python scripts that save you hours"
4. Password rules that lie ("complex" vs "long")
5. "Your developer vs a hacker — who wins?" (roleplay, two of you — you ARE a duo, use that)
6. Reacting to a data-breach headline ("here's how it actually happened")
7. Free tools audit: "Is this SaaS safe to connect to your business?"
8. Before/after of an ugly dashboard you rebuilt
9. "What $X gets you: freelancer vs agency vs us"
10. Client result story in 30 seconds

## Engagement rules (these get you clients, not just likes)

- **Reply to every comment within an hour** of posting (the algorithm repays you)
- **Set an Instagram Quick Reply** for the word "PROJECT" → price range + book a chat link
- **End every caption with ONE question** (see `03-captions-and-hashtags.md`)
- **DM new followers** who look like founders/business owners: thanks + one free tip. Not a pitch.
- **Collab-post** (`Invite collaborator`) with every client — their audience sees the case study
